2 · Camera on the dial gauge

  1. Pick the camera that shows the gauge, then click Open.
  2. If the digits are upside down, set Rotate to 180°.
  3. Click Draw box and drag over the gauge display in the picture.
  4. Set how many digits your gauge shows after the point (0.99 → 2).
  5. Click Test read. The number must match the gauge.
More (mirror, clear box, vision server)
The key comes from MOTORON_VISION_KEY, the same as the MOTORON bench.

3 · Set the centre

  1. Point the lever straight up and hold it still.
  2. Click the button. This position becomes 2048 (saved in the servo).

Do this again after every firmware flash.

4 · Mark the two positions

  1. Click Release. The motor goes limp.
  2. Move the lever by hand away from the gauge, to the end. Click Mark away end.
  3. Move it towards the gauge until it touches and the gauge shows a reading (needle mid-travel). Hold it there and click Mark gauge position.
  4. Click Save.

Change firmware

  1. Finish the tests on the current firmware. Keep the servo connected (step 1).
  2. Pick the firmware, type FLASH and click Upload to servo. It takes about a minute. The bench backs up the servo settings first and reconnects when it's done.
  3. Then redo 3 Centre and 4 Positions. Keep the run settings the same.

      
Use stable power, keep only this servo on the bus, and don't unplug anything or close the window during the upload.

      
Restore servo settings

      
Use this if the servo shows Settings wiped (model 9.255, limits 65535, voltage alarm, 0 V / 10 °C readings)
        although the supply is on. It writes a saved settings snapshot of this servo back, then restarts it.
        A snapshot is saved automatically before every upload. ID and baud are never changed. Redo Centre and Positions afterwards.
